WHY 1MPACT EXISTS
It's not just about what we create. It's about the people we reach.

Life can get confusing. Faith can get complicated. Purpose can feel distant.

Sometimes you simply need somebody willing to listen without judging you.

1MPACT exists to create a space where young people can talk, ask questions, grow, discover purpose and take their next step with Christ.

Whether you know exactly what you need or you don't even know where to start, you're welcome here.

A SPACE TO BE HEARD

Need someone to talk to?

Sometimes you don't need a lecture. You just need someone willing to listen.

You don't have to know what to say.

Tell us what's on your heart. It can be about your faith, purpose, relationships, school, family, personal struggles or something you can't even explain yet.


You can simply say: "I don't know what I need. I just need someone to talk to."
